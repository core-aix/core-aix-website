Prof. Shiqiang Wang spoke today at the [Devon AI Summit 2026](https://www.eventbrite.ie/e/devon-ai-summit-2026-tickets-1999498667339) at Sandy Park in Exeter, hosted by the University of Exeter.

Shiqiang's talk introduced **agentic AI** and the iterative loop behind it: a user sets a goal, a large language model reasons and plans, the agent calls tools to gather information or take actions, and the model analyses the results before repeating the cycle or giving a final answer. The talk also looked at where businesses are putting these systems to use, such as customer service.
